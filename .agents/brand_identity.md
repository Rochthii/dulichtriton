# BRAND IDENTITY — Chatbot Du Lịch Tri Tôn AI

## Brand Name
**Tri Tôn AI** / **TRIA** (chatbot name)

## Brand Promise
"Hỏi một câu — biết cả Tri Tôn"

## Brand Values
1. **Chân thực (Authenticity):** Every fact verified from real sources
2. **Địa phương (Local-first):** Celebrate Tri Tôn's unique Khmer-Kinh identity
3. **Thông minh (Intelligence):** AI that truly understands Vietnamese tourism context
4. **Hiện đại (Modernity):** Video-first, map-first, mobile-first experience

## Target Users
| Persona | Description |
|---|---|
| Khách du lịch nội địa | Vietnamese domestic tourists, age 22-45, mobile-first |
| Tây ba lô | Foreign backpackers seeking authentic experiences |
| Photographer/Content Creator | TikTok/IG creators scouting scenic spots |
| Family traveler | Weekend trips from HCMC/Cần Thơ |
| Researcher/Journalist | Local culture, Khmer heritage documentation |

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep Khmer Gold `#D4A017`
- **Secondary:** Mountain Mist `#2C5F6B` (rice field green-blue)
- **Accent:** Lotus Red `#C0392B`
- **Background:** Dark Slate `#1A1A2E`
- **Text:** Warm White `#F5F0E8`

## Typography
- **Display:** Be Vietnam Pro — proud, modern Vietnamese identity
- **Body:** Nunito — approachable, readable at small sizes
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono — for technical/data displays

## Logo Concept
- Silhouette of Cô Tô Mountain over rice fields
- Stylized "T" representing both "Tri Tôn" and "Technology"
- Soft golden glow suggesting sunrise over Bảy Núi mountains

## Key Messaging (Vietnamese)
- "Khám phá Tri Tôn không cần hướng dẫn viên"
- "82 địa điểm xác thực — 0 thông tin sai lệch"
- "Nhìn video, xem bản đồ, đặt lịch ngay trên AI"

## Unique Differentiators
1. Only AI platform dedicated exclusively to Tri Tôn tourism
2. 82 verified local spots — curated by locals, not algorithms
3. TikTok video integration — see before you go
4. Khmer cultural heritage deep knowledge
5. Offline-capable progressive web app (PWA)
6. Scenic road guides (con đường tơ lụa, tuyến tránh Tri Tôn)
